The Stitcher Trilogy

Bartosz Osiej · Dark Literary Horror · English · Kindle Unlimited

About the series

Three dark literary horror novels about obsession, silk, and the thin line between art and madness. A detective who learns to see beauty in what terrifies him. A curator who loves her collection too much. And the thread that connects them — fraying.

Theme: what art costs the artist. Motif: buttons. Keep the lights on.

About the author

Bartosz Osiej writes crime horror with a systems engineer's eye. He is a Polish developer working in Rust, eBPF and the Linux kernel, and he brings that same precision to fiction: obsession, cryptography, and the quiet, mechanical ways people hurt each other. He lives in Poland and writes in English.

The books

Book One

The Stitcher

Marcus Delgado was a good detective. Fifteen years on the force. Twenty-three homicides. Four cold cases. Then he walked into apartment 4B and found Mrs. Harlow surrounded by dolls made from human skin. Now Marcus is obsessed — not with the killing, but with the creation. A literary thriller about the dark side of creation and the thin line between art and madness.

ASIN B0HHY39314 · Kindle eBook & paperback · Kindle Unlimited · English · Published 2026-09-05 · amazon.com/dp/B0HHY39314

Book Two

The Curator

Pia Redfern inherits a private museum: three rooms of taxidermy, shells, and curiosities collected over a century. The will forbids her from opening the storage room at the far end. She opens it anyway. Sleeping Beauty, 1912. Preserved in glass. Not dead. What does a museum do when its exhibits start looking back?

ASIN B0HHXT2ZRZ · Kindle eBook · Kindle Unlimited · English · 2026 · amazon.com/dp/B0HHXT2ZRZ

Book Three

The Reckoning

The thread is fraying. Someone is cutting it. The evidence is vanishing from the museum. The dolls are appearing in new places — homes, cars, waiting rooms where nobody hangs coats. A woman without a name is taking the pieces apart to find the pattern underneath. The end of the trilogy.
